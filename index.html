What We Do

Three focus areas. One global vision.

We apply our expertise to transform an idea into a viable, scalable business, integrating technology, strategy and growth under one roof, focusing on three high-potential areas:

Technology

We support ideas that use technology as the engine to solve problems, optimize operations and create new business opportunities. This includes artificial intelligence projects, process automation, digital platforms, custom software and development, technology integrations, digital presence and infrastructure.

Digital Marketing

We drive ideas focused on building brands, growing audiences, sharing knowledge and generating revenue through digital channels. This area covers content and monetization projects on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook, as well as e-books, online courses, digital communities and strategies to promote and sell products or services.

Finance & Investment

We support the development of digital tools and solutions for financial guidance for individuals and companies. These ideas can focus on diagnosing financial situations, organizing budgets, improving habits, managing debt, strengthening credit profiles and facilitating the evaluation of investment alternatives.

How We Work

A clear, five-phase path from idea to global business.

Every engagement follows the same methodical process. So you always know what phase you're in, what it takes to move forward, and what you'll have in hand at the end of it.

  1. 01

    Idea Diagnosis

    “Does this idea have real potential to become a business?”

    We evaluate the proposal, the problem, the customer, the differentiation and the global reach.

    Result: Initial diagnosis and recommendation.

  2. 02

    Market Validation

    “Is there a real commercial opportunity here?”

    Research, competition analysis, demand testing, interviews and willingness-to-pay signals.

    Result: Evidence of the commercial opportunity.

  3. 03

    Business Design

    “What does a viable business around this idea look like — and do we build it together?”

    Revenue model, value proposition, cost structure, channels and strategy. If it makes sense for both sides, this is also where we define a potential Venture Partnership: roles, responsibilities, equity participation, IP and profit distribution.

    Result: A viable business model — and, where applicable, a partnership agreement to move into development.

  4. 04

    Development & Launch

    “How do we turn the validated idea into a real business?”

    Brand, prototype or MVP, digital presence, automation, marketing and sales.

    Result: A business ready to be tested in the market.

  5. 05

    Global Scaling

    “How do we grow and expand a business that already works?”

    Optimization, geographic expansion, new channels, alliances and investment readiness.

    Result: A business prepared to grow.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything founders usually ask before developing their idea with us.

What is a Venture Studio, and how is DBL different from a consultant or an accelerator?

A consultant advises; an accelerator mentors companies that already exist. A Venture Studio like DBL does both and more — we validate the idea, help design and build the business alongside you, and for the highest-potential projects, we can join as strategic or equity partners. We share in the execution, not just the advice.

Who is DBL for?

Entrepreneurs with an idea but no validated business model yet; early-stage startups that need strategy, development and market access; professionals with sector expertise who want to turn it into a company; companies creating new business units or digital products; and founders open to a strategic alliance or equity partnership with DBL.

What happens after I submit my idea in "Build With Us"?

Our team reviews every submission personally. If there's a potential fit, we'll reach out within 2 business days to schedule an initial conversation — the starting point of Phase 1, Idea Diagnosis.

Do I have to give up equity to work with DBL?

No. Equity partnership is only one possible path, reserved for select high-potential projects where both sides agree it makes sense — and it's always discussed and defined explicitly during the Business Design phase, never assumed. Many engagements move forward as straightforward paid consulting or development work instead.

Will my idea be kept confidential?

We treat every submission with discretion, and only a small internal team reviews it. That said, submitting the form does not by itself create a legally binding NDA — if you'd like a signed confidentiality agreement before sharing sensitive details, just ask us for one before your first call.

What if my idea isn't validated, or isn't the right fit for DBL?

We'll tell you directly and explain why — that's part of our process. A "no" from us at the Diagnosis or Validation phase is far cheaper than finding out after months of building on your own.

Which industries or focus areas does DBL work in?

We concentrate on three areas: Technology (AI, automation, custom software and digital platforms), Digital Marketing (brand building, content and audience monetization) and Finance & Investment (digital financial guidance tools). Most projects combine more than one.

Do I need to already have a company, or can I start with just an idea?

Just an idea is enough to start. Idea Diagnosis, our first phase, exists specifically to evaluate raw ideas before any company structure is in place.

How much does it cost to work with DBL?

It depends on the phase and type of engagement — some phases are offered as fixed-scope paid work, others (for select projects) as an equity or revenue-share partnership. We'll walk you through the options that make sense for your project after the initial diagnosis.

How do I get started?

Fill out the form in the Build With Us section with a description of your idea or project. That's it — we take it from there.
